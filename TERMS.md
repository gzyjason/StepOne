## Description of service

StepOne is a walking app that helps you track distance and progress toward Trip milestones, organized by categories you choose. Some features work fully offline; if you create an account, your Trip progress can sync across devices via Firebase. Reminder notifications, when enabled, are scheduled locally on your device.
