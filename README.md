# StepOne
![StepOne logo](https://github.com/gzyjason/StepOne/blob/trip-deck-rotation-and-empty-state/screenshots/logo.png "StepOne")
## A free, open source app for building motivation with small achievable steps

*This app was built **primarily** through vibe coding, and was intended as a helpful personal project for learning the basics of agentic coding.*
*Please excuse the messy code and implementations for this reason*

## What is StepOne?

StepOne is an **open source** app written in **Swift** that aims to help people who are feeling unmotivated for various reasons find their motivation through a game-like interface that guides them to do small, achievable steps (called a _Trip_ in the app) like taking a small walk or even just doing some light stretching, with the intention of having these small steps lead to a chain of bigger actions and eventually a reignited passion and motivation.

The app is currently developed for **iOS only**, with a _potential_ Android version coming in the future.

## Why did I create StepOne?

I have personally struggled with low motivation due to a series of mental and physical health issues. One of the things that helped me the most during that time was my therapist's teaching that I needed to start with tiny actions that could be completed within minutes if not seconds, and that these actions would quickly build up my self-esteem and lead to greater achievements.

It is my goal to use every personal project I make - under the name [MorSo](morso.one "MorSo Homepage"), or Mortal Software - to solve _some_ kind of problem that have bothered me in the past. StepOne is just a start.

## How does StepOne work?

On the homepage of the app, there are 1-3 user-switchable **_Trip Types_**, each containing many different trips that can either be **completed** by swiping up or **discarded** by swiping down. User can also swipe left/right to **choose** from the wide range of different available trips.

Each completed trip add a certain amount of **_Distance_** to the user's profile. The exact amount of Distance added depends on the difficulty and complexity of the Trip being completed.

![Homepage screenshot](https://github.com/gzyjason/StepOne/blob/trip-deck-rotation-and-empty-state/screenshots/home.png "Homepage")

There is also a _**Journey**_ button on the homepage that can be pressed on to check the total Distance completed by the user, as well as a list of **_Milestones_** that can be unlocked with enough accumulated Distance.

![Journey screenshot](https://github.com/gzyjason/StepOne/blob/trip-deck-rotation-and-empty-state/screenshots/journey.png "Journey")

**That's all you need to get started! Enjoy!**
